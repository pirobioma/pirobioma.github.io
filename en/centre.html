Centre for Integrative Ecology

The Universidad de Talca research centre where the Pirobioma project is carried out.

Connecting scales by modelling ecological processes.

The Centre for Integrative Ecology (CEI, Centro de Ecología Integrativa) at the Universidad de Talca brings together researchers, students and professionals who study how ecosystems work, from soil microorganisms and molecules to landscapes and climate.

Its work combines fieldwork, laboratory research and modelling to understand processes that unfold at very different scales, and to connect them with one another.

  • Microbiomes
  • Environmental microbiology
  • Molecular ecology
  • Biogeochemical cycles
  • Plant ecophysiology
  • Biological invasions
  • Climate change
  • Ecosystem services
  • Food security
  • Astrobiology

The Pirobioma project intersects with several of these lines: soil microbiomes, biological invasions and plant ecology after fire.
