Pirobioma del Maule

Bacteria and fungi isolated from wildfire-affected soils in the Maule Region (Chile) as a biotechnological resource for post-fire restoration.

Explore the catalogue About the project

FONDECYT Initiation project No. 11240628 · Universidad de Talca

Explore the catalogue Aspergillus fumigatus · H76 Fungi in culture Peribacillus simplex · B27 Rhodococcus sp. · B5 Penicillium spinulosum · H62

The catalogue gathers 139 strains grown on selective media with burned-wood charcoal as the sole carbon source: 74 fungi and 65 bacteria, isolated from soils of both native forest (Nothofagus alessandrii and N. glauca) and Pinus radiata plantations burned in the 2017 and 2023 megafires.

Each strain, seen in several ways

Each record in the catalogue shows the colony on the plate and its image under the microscope (some stained), along with its taxonomy, its DNA sequence (16S or ITS) and an indicator of its ability to grow using charcoal (burned wood) as the sole carbon source. The example shows Trichoderma reesei (H16), a fungus isolated from burned native forest.
